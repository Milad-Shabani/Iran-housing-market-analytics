# Data dictionary

## Raw (`data/raw/`, committed, re-downloadable with `scripts/fetch_sources.py`)

### `divar_1m/` — aggregates of the Divar official 1M-ad dataset (ODbL)
Published by [maminigder/Iran-Real-Estate-Market-Analysis](https://github.com/maminigder/Iran-Real-Estate-Market-Analysis)
from [divarofficial/real_estate_ads](https://huggingface.co/datasets/divarofficial/real_estate_ads).
Core sample: 516,947 residential **sale** ads, 420 cities, listing months 2021-02 to 2025-03 (mostly
May–Dec 2024). Prices are asking prices in toman (consistent with the Central Bank's toman figures
for the same months).

| File | Grain | Key columns |
|---|---|---|
| `city_summary.csv` | city (420) | `listings`, `median_asking_price`, `median_price_per_sqm`, `median_building_size_sqm`, `median_rooms` |
| `tehran_neighborhood_summary.csv` | Tehran neighbourhood (345) | medians and quartiles of price per m², size, rooms, construction year (Jalali), median lat/lon, amenity shares |
| `tehran_neighborhood_uncertainty.csv` | neighbourhood (104 with ≥250 ads) | 95% CI of the median |
| `major_cities_monthly.csv`, `major_cities_monthly_index.csv` | city × month (5 cities) | raw median, composition-adjusted index (May 2024 = 100) |
| `amenity_summary.csv`, `construction_period_summary.csv` | national | medians by amenity and by construction decade |

### `divar_2021/tehran_listings_raw.csv` — 3,479 individual Tehran ads
`Area` (text, thousands separators), `Room`, `Parking`, `Warehouse`, `Elevator` (booleans),
`Address` (192 free-text neighbourhood labels), `Price` (toman), `Price(USD)` (= Price / 30,000).
Undated; see methodology §3.

### `official/cbi_tehran_monthly.csv` — Central Bank of Iran
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `jalali_year`, `jalali_month` | Solar Hijri month |
| `price_per_m2_toman` | mean price per m² of Tehran residential transactions |
| `transactions` | number of transactions (1400/06–1401/09 only) |
| `source`, `source_url` | where the figure was taken from |
| `quality` | `official_series_transcribed`, `scale_inferred_crosschecked`, `secondary_report`, `rounded_secondary_report` |

### `official/kilid_tehran_monthly.csv` — Kilid.com
Monthly Tehran listing indicator, 1404/06–1405/05, toman per m². Methodology not published in
full; treated as a platform signal.
