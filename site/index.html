4Tehran's price over time, and the next 12 months

The Central Bank's monthly average transaction price (1395–1403) and Kilid's listing indicator (1404–1405). The forecast method was chosen by testing five methods on 35 historical forecast origins.

Scale

The 2 October 2026 Divar collection (17,175 listings in 19 of 22 districts) is excluded from the Tehran trend because coverage was incomplete. The original observation is retained in the data history.

Year-on-year change, official series

Tehran's price moves in jumps (1397, 1399, 1402) separated by plateaus, which is why momentum forecasts fail.

Which forecasting method actually works?

Mean absolute % error by horizon, rolling-origin backtest on the official series. Lower is better; best per column in bold.
